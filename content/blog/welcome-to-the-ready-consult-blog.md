Welcome to the first post on the new Ready Consult blog. We built this space to publish the **operating playbooks** behind our client work — the AI workflows, content engines, paid-ad teardowns, and growth experiments we run for ourselves and for the companies we partner with.

## Why we built an AI-first growth blog in 2026

We sit at a strange intersection: most agencies sell deliverables, most "AI thought leaders" sell screenshots. We do neither. We run growth systems, and we want to publish what works — and what doesn't.

If you've ever wanted a peek at:

- How a real AI content engine is wired together end-to-end
- What a 72-hour test loop on a paid campaign actually looks like in the wild
- The frameworks we use to take a $1M–$20M company from "we should do content" to "content drives 30% of pipeline"

That's what's coming.

## What kind of growth content will The Ready Consult publish?

Three editorial lanes, each with its own cadence:

**AI workflows.** The n8n graphs, prompts, and guardrails we use to ship daily content, run SEO ops, and keep the system honest. Every workflow we publish has run in production for at least four weeks before we write about it.

**Growth experiments.** Honest write-ups of what we tried, what worked, and what didn't. Including the campaigns that lost money — because survivorship bias is the enemy of learning.

**Content systems.** Distribution playbooks, repurposing flows, and the operating systems that let one client match the output of a five-person agency.

## How should founders and operators use this blog?

If you're a founder or operator, **start with the growth experiments**. They'll tell you whether our worldview matches yours before you commit to a deeper read.

If you're a marketer or in-house growth lead, **start with the AI workflows**. You can copy them outright; we don't gatekeep tooling.

If you're a fellow consultant, **the content systems lane is where the meatiest material lives** — operating systems over hot takes.

## What's the fastest way to put these playbooks to work?

Two paths, depending on where you are:

If you have an in-house team, **start with the workflow templates** as you find them. Copy the n8n graphs, fork the prompts, swap your variables. Most posts include the JSON files attached.

If you'd rather have us run the system for you, **book a discovery call** from the homepage. We'll talk through your current motion, where the leverage actually is, and whether an engagement makes sense.

Either way: subscribe. We'll keep showing up in your inbox every week with playbooks you can copy.
